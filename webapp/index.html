What maps from disk: loop_state.json gives the ledger, the autonomy gates and the consult list; decisions/decision-NN.json gives the type, reasoning, rejected alternatives, allowed set and policy advice; analysis.json gives per-arm target and QC; the approved protocol gives the parameter table with provenance tags. Only the live agent states come from your Omnigent session rather than from disk.

Demo data replays a synthetic CAR-T process-development loop used to test this interface. The gene name, the source paper, the reactor and every number are invented; the decision documents are scripted stand-ins for the orchestrator's reasoning. Nothing here is clinical or manufacturing guidance.
