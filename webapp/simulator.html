    Runs the conditions above twice: once as the control, once with an effect you assume — how much faster or slower the cells grow, and how much better or worse they differentiate (×1 = no effect). An edited line carries it through the whole process; an added factor (a new cytokine, small molecule or supplement the reactor has no term for) acts only in the stage you give it. The curves are that assumption played through the reactor, labelled as one — not a prediction of what the gene or the factor does.
